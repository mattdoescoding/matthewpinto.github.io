01Academic planning · Full-stack application

Chronoly

A Rutgers course-planning platform that helps students navigate degree requirements and map a path to graduation.

I built the planning workflows, course data model, and authenticated APIs, bringing complex prerequisites and individual academic histories into one application.

  • Next.js
  • TypeScript
  • PostgreSQL
  • Prisma
  • Auth.js
Inside the build +

The platform models approximately 30,000 active course records and supported 30 beta users and 150 generated plans.

I added JWT validation, rate limiting, ownership checks, session invalidation, and database persistence, supported by automated testing and CI/CD.

Planning logic includes graph traversal, transitive closure, topological ordering, course normalization, and choose-N requirement evaluation. I reduced worst-case transcript parsing time from 26.47 seconds to 134 milliseconds.

02Small business · Booking & Operations

A&M Detail Co.

A custom booking and operations platform for the mobile vehicle detailing business I co-founded.

I built live scheduling, owner approvals, and automated emails, then integrated live Zoho CRM metrics to track service duration and material consumption and guide operational improvements.

  • Next.js
  • TypeScript
  • PostgreSQL
  • Prisma
  • Auth.js
  • Resend
  • Zoho CRM
Visit deployed website ↗
Inside the build +

Scheduling uses live availability, variable slot holds, row locking, unique slot keys, and replay-safe submissions to coordinate bookings.

An authenticated owner dashboard uses JWTs, Auth.js, and bcrypt. Resend and webhooks automated 36 approval, denial, and reminder emails to 12 clients, with failure notifications.

Live Zoho CRM metrics map operational data and flag service durations and material consumption. These insights guided workflow and resource improvements, reducing average production time by 37.5% and material cost by 70%.

The business generated over $3,800 in cash revenue in approximately two months, detailing 21 vehicles across 18 appointments.
